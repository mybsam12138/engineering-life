# Questions to Ask the Interviewer

---

## Your 3 Planned Questions

### 1. Role Clarity
> *"What exactly is the role you are looking for right now, and what would my key responsibilities be?"*

- Helps you understand if the role matches your expectations
- Shows you are serious and want clarity before committing

### 2. Next Steps & Timeline
> *"What are the next steps in the hiring process? I hope to be notified as soon as possible regarding whether I am a good fit."*

- Shows enthusiasm and eagerness
- Gives you a clear timeline to follow up if needed

### 3. Job Nature & Stability
> *"Could you tell me more about the nature of this position — is it a long-term stable role, project-based, or contract?"*

- Important for your career planning
- Helps you evaluate if it fits your goals

---

## Recommended Additional Questions (Pick 1–2)

### About Growth
> *"What does career progression look like for this role? Are there opportunities to grow into architecture or leadership?"*

### About the Team
> *"Can you tell me about the team I'd be working with and how technical decisions are made?"*

### About Culture
> *"What do you enjoy most about working here?"*

### About Expectations
> *"What does success look like in this role after the first 6 months?"*

---

## Key Tips

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Prepare at least 2–3 questions | Say "No, I have no questions" |
| Ask about growth and the role | Ask about salary too early |
| Show genuine curiosity | Ask things easily found on their website |
| End on a positive, enthusiastic note | Ask too many questions at once |

---

## Quick Reminder

> Asking good questions shows you are **engaged, prepared, and thinking like an employee** — not just someone looking for any job.
