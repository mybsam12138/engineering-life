# Business Evaluation Checklist (Company Analysis)

## 🧠 One-line Summary
Evaluate a company from business aspects by understanding:
👉 **Scale + Revenue Model + Core Business + Market Position + Growth Potential**

---

## 📊 1. Company Scale

### What to check
- Number of employees
- Revenue size
- Number of users/customers
- Global vs local presence

### Why it matters
- Large scale → stability, resources
- Small scale → flexibility, faster growth

---

## 💰 2. Revenue Model (How it earns money)

### Common models
- Subscription (SaaS)
- Transaction fees
- Licensing
- Advertising
- Insurance premiums (your domain)

### Key questions
- Who pays?
- How often do they pay?
- Is revenue predictable?

---

## 🧩 3. Core Business (What it mainly does)

### What to check
- Main products/services
- Target customers (B2B / B2C)
- Key value proposition

### Example
- Insurance system → policy management, pricing, claims

---

## 🏆 4. Market Position

### What to check
- Market share
- Competitors
- Competitive advantage (moat)

### Examples of advantage
- Technology
- Brand
- Data
- Regulation barriers

---

## 📈 5. Growth Potential

### What to check
- Industry growth rate
- Company expansion plans
- New products or markets

### Signals
- Hiring expansion
- Investment rounds
- New business lines

---

## ⚖️ 6. Profitability & Sustainability

### What to check
- Profit vs loss
- Cost structure
- Burn rate (for startups)

### Why it matters
- Profitable → stable
- High burn → risky but high growth

---

## 🧠 7. Business Complexity

### What to check
- Domain complexity (insurance, finance = high)
- Business rules complexity
- Integration with other systems

### Why it matters
- High complexity → better learning
- But harder to maintain

---

## 🚀 8. Product & Innovation

### What to check
- Product maturity
- Innovation level
- Use of technology (AI, cloud, etc.)

---

## 🌍 9. Industry & External Factors

### What to check
- Regulation level
- Economic sensitivity
- Industry stability

---

## 🚩 Red Flags

- Unclear revenue model
- No clear product direction
- Heavy dependence on one client
- Declining industry
- Constant pivot without strategy

---

## ✅ Green Flags

- Clear and sustainable revenue model
- Strong market position
- Growing industry
- Scalable business model
- Diversified customer base

---

## 🧠 Final Takeaway

👉 A good company = **clear business + sustainable revenue + growth potential**
