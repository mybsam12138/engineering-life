# How to Evaluate a Company (Software Engineer Perspective)

## 🧠 One-line Summary

Choosing a company is about evaluating **technology, team, process, and growth potential**, not just salary.

---

## 📊 Key Evaluation Aspects

### 1. 💰 Compensation & Benefits
- Salary level (base + bonus)
- Stock / equity
- Benefits (insurance, vacation, etc.)

---

### 2. 🧑‍💻 Technical Environment
- Tech stack (Java, Spring Boot, cloud, etc.)
- Code quality (clean code, standards)
- Architecture (monolith vs microservices)
- Use of modern tools (CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes)

---

### 3. 🏗️ Engineering Culture
- Code review process
- Testing culture (unit test, integration test)
- Refactoring attitude
- Technical debt management

---

### 4. 🔄 Development Process
- Agile vs Waterfall
- Scrum / Kanban usage
- Release frequency
- Feedback loop efficiency

---

### 5. 👥 Team Quality
- Skill level of teammates
- Presence of senior engineers
- Mentorship opportunities
- Communication style

---

### 6. 📈 Growth & Learning
- Learning opportunities
- Exposure to system design
- Career path clarity
- Internal mobility

---

### 7. 🏢 Business & Domain
- Industry (finance, insurance, SaaS, etc.)
- Stability of business
- Complexity of domain
- Long-term potential

---

### 8. ⚖️ Work-Life Balance
- Working hours
- Overtime culture
- On-call requirements

---

### 9. 🧭 Management Style
- Decision-making process
- Transparency
- Respect for engineers
- Micromanagement vs autonomy

---

### 10. 🚀 Impact & Ownership
- Can you influence system design?
- Ownership of modules
- Opportunity to make decisions

---

## 🧠 Red Flags 🚩

- No code review
- No testing culture
- Frequent production issues
- Constant overtime
- Unclear requirements
- High employee turnover

---

## 🧠 Green Flags ✅

- Clean architecture
- Strong engineering culture
- Continuous learning environment
- Respect for technical decisions
- Stable and clear roadmap

---

## 🧠 Final Takeaway

A good company is not just about salary, but about:

👉 **Growth + Technology + Culture + Stability**
