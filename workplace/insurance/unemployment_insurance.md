# Shenzhen Unemployment Insurance (Summary)

## 1. Eligibility (Basic)
- Must have contributed to unemployment insurance for **≥ 1 year**
- Must be involuntarily unemployed
- Must complete unemployment registration

---

## 2. Monthly Benefit Amount

- Typically around **80% of Shenzhen minimum wage** 
- In practice across China: about **70%–80% of minimum wage** 

### Example (Shenzhen)
- Shenzhen minimum wage ≈ **RMB 2,360/month (2025)** 
- Estimated unemployment benefit:
    - **~RMB 1,650 – 1,900/month**

---

## 3. Duration (Months You Can Receive)

Depends on total contribution years:

| Contribution Years | Benefit Duration |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| 1–5 years         | Up to ~12 months |
| 5–10 years        | Up to ~18 months |
| ≥10 years         | Up to ~24 months (max) |

- National guideline: **3–24 months total** 

---

## 4. Other Benefits

During unemployment:
- Medical insurance is **paid by the unemployment fund** 
- Access to:
    - Job placement services
    - Training programs
    - Possible subsidies

---

## 5. Notes

- You can claim multiple times if you meet conditions each time
- Amount is linked to **minimum wage**, so it changes when the city updates it 

---

## Quick Summary

- **Amount:** ~RMB 1,600–1,900/month
- **Duration:** 3–24 months (based on contribution years)
- **Core rule:** More years contributed → longer duration
