# Job Nature — A Complete Guide

---

## Overview

Job nature refers to the **type of employment contract** you sign with a company. In China's software industry, there are three main types. Understanding them helps you evaluate stability, benefits, and your legal rights before accepting an offer.

---

## 1. Project-Based

### What It Is
Employment tied to a **specific project**. The contract ends when the project is completed or the deliverable is accepted.

### Key Characteristics
- Termination condition = project completion or client acceptance
- Duration is uncertain — depends on project timeline
- Common in outsourcing, system integration, and software contracting

### Compensation When Terminated
| Situation | Compensation |
|-----------|-------------|
| Project completes normally | N |
| Company terminates early without notice | N+1 |
| Company terminates illegally | 2N |

### Watch Out For
- Termination conditions must be **clearly defined** in the contract
- Vague wording like "project completion" without a defined acceptance standard is risky
- If termination conditions are unclear, try to negotiate a fixed-term contract instead

---

## 2. Contract-Based / Fixed-Term

### What It Is
Employment with a **clearly defined start and end date**, such as 1 year or 2 years.

### Key Characteristics
- Most common employment type in China's software industry
- Fully protected under Chinese Labor Law
- Includes social insurance and housing fund
- Can be renewed upon expiry
- After **2 consecutive renewals**, employee has the right to request an unlimited-term contract

### Compensation When Terminated
| Situation | Compensation |
|-----------|-------------|
| Contract expires, company does not renew | N |
| Company renews but with worse conditions, employee refuses | N |
| Company terminates early with 30 days notice | N |
| Company terminates early without 30 days notice | N+1 |
| Company terminates illegally (no valid reason) | 2N |

### Key Point
> Contract expiry is considered a **legally valid reason** for termination — it does not trigger 2N compensation.

---

## 3. Unlimited / Permanent

### What It Is
Employment with **no end date**. The contract continues indefinitely until either party terminates it under legal conditions.

### Key Characteristics
- Most stable form of employment
- Hardest for the company to legally terminate
- Company must have valid legal grounds to end the contract
- Usually offered to senior staff or after long service

### How You Get It
- After **2 consecutive fixed-term contract renewals**
- After working continuously for **10 years** at the same company
- By negotiating it directly when joining

### Compensation When Terminated
| Situation | Compensation |
|-----------|-------------|
| Company terminates with valid reason, with notice | N |
| Company terminates with valid reason, without notice | N+1 |
| Company terminates without valid reason (illegal) | 2N |

---

## Compensation Reference Table

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| N | 1 month salary per year worked |
| +1 | Extra 1 month because company did not give 30 days notice |
| 2N | Double compensation for illegal termination |

> Salary base = average monthly salary over the last 12 months

## Important Distinction — Labor Contract vs Service Contract
```
+-----------------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
|                             | Labor Contract   | Service Contract          |
+=============================+==================+===========================+
| Protected by Labor Law      | Yes              | No                        |
+-----------------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
| Social insurance included   | Yes              | No                        |
+-----------------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
| Termination compensation    | Yes              | No                        |
+-----------------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
| Common for                  | All 3 types above| Freelancers, temp workers |
+-----------------------------+------------------+---------------------------+
```
> Always verify whether you are signing a **labor contract** or a **service contract** — two characters difference, but your rights are completely different.
