# 🧠 Why Important Events Make You Nervous (and Reduce Deep Thinking)

## 📌 Core Idea

> When you face something important (e.g., an interview), your brain switches from **thinking mode** to **survival mode**, which reduces your ability to think deeply.

---

# ⚙️ Why This Happens

## 1. Amygdala detects "threat"

- The **amygdala** acts as an emotional alarm system  
- It interprets important events as:
  - Evaluation
  - Uncertainty
  - Possible failure

👉 Result:
- Triggers stress response even if there is no real danger

---

## 2. Stress hormones are released

- **Cortisol ↑**
- **Adrenaline ↑**

👉 Effects:
- Heart rate increases
- Alertness increases
- Body prepares for "fight or flight"

---

## 3. Prefrontal cortex is suppressed

- The **prefrontal cortex** is responsible for:
  - Logical thinking
  - Planning
  - Structured communication

Under stress:
- Neural activity is reduced  
- Cognitive control becomes weaker  

👉 Result:
- Harder to organize thoughts  
- Harder to explain clearly  
- “I know it, but I can’t say it”

---

## 4. Working memory decreases

- Working memory = your mental RAM

Under stress:
- Capacity becomes smaller  
- Harder to hold multiple ideas at once  

👉 Result:
- Lose structure when speaking  
- Forget steps in reasoning  

---

## 5. Brain shifts to "fast mode"

- Prefers:
  - Habits
  - Memorized patterns
- Avoids:
  - Deep reasoning
  - Complex analysis

👉 Result:
- You rely on instinct instead of thinking

---

# 🧩 Summary of the Mechanism

```
Important event
      ↓
Amygdala detects threat
      ↓
Stress hormones released
      ↓
Prefrontal cortex suppressed
      ↓
Working memory reduced
      ↓
Deep thinking ability decreases
```

---

# 💡 Key Insight

> You are not becoming less capable — your brain is temporarily switching modes.

---

# 🛠️ How to Deal With It

## 1. Separate "Preparation" and "Execution"

- Set a clear boundary:
  - Time for preparation
  - Time to stop thinking

👉 Helps reduce mental overload

---

## 2. Use a "parking note"

- Write down anxious thoughts:
  - Things to review
  - Questions to prepare

👉 Tells your brain:
> “This is handled”

---

## 3. Lower psychological pressure

Instead of:
- ❌ “I must perform well”

Use:
- ✅ “This is just one iteration”

👉 Reduces stress response

---

## 4. Prepare structured patterns

Since stress reduces deep thinking:

👉 Prepare in advance:
- Key project explanations  
- System design frameworks  
- Standard answer structures  

---

## 5. Use gradual focus switching

When nervous:
1. Start with light tasks (review notes)
2. Then move to deep work

👉 Helps your brain transition smoothly

---

## 6. Accept the feeling

- Don’t fight nervousness
- Recognize it as normal

👉 Acceptance reduces intensity

---

# 🧠 Final Mental Model

- Calm → **Architect mode (deep thinking)**
- Stress → **Soldier mode (fast execution)**

👉 Strategy:
> Train in calm state → execute under stress

---

# 📌 One Sentence to Remember

> Nervousness is not weakness — it is a biological response you can manage.
