# 💰 Personal Income Tax Return Summary (China)

## 🧾 1. Key Concept

Tax deductions (like 36000 or 18000) are:

> ❗ NOT direct cash refunds  
> 👉 They reduce your **taxable income**, not your tax directly

---

## 🧠 2. Tax Calculation Logic

### Step 1: Calculate Taxable Income

Taxable Income =
Total Income
- 60000 (Basic deduction)
- Social Insurance & Housing Fund
- Special Additional Deductions (e.g., rent, elderly support)

---

### Step 2: Apply Tax Rate

Tax = Taxable Income × Tax Rate

---

## 💡 3. Real Value of Deductions

Formula:

Tax Saved = Deduction × Tax Rate

---

## 📊 4. Your Case (Approx. 20% Tax Bracket)

### 🏠 Rent Deduction

18000 × 20% = 3600 RMB

👉 💰 Saves about **3600 RMB tax**
